01

Overview

Monkey Garden is a medieval kingdom inhabited entirely by monkeys. They live in harmony with the forest, building their homes in trees and on the ground, and cultivating the land with care.

Though small in size, their kingdom is rich in culture and tradition, guided by honour, curiosity and a deep respect for the natural world.

02

Origins

The ancestors of Monkey Garden settled in the forests generations ago, seeking shelter and freedom from the conflicts of the outside world.

Over time, they built a kingdom of their own amongst the trees.

03

Society & Culture

Monkey Garden is a deeply community-driven kingdom. They value knowledge, artistry and balance with nature. Their culture is rich with storytelling, music and rituals that honour their ancestors and the forest.

04

Notable Features

  • Treehouse districts built high among the canopy.
  • Fertile farmlands carved from forest clearings.
  • Skilled craftsmen, known for woodwork and weaving.
  • Strong spiritual connection to the forest and its creatures.
